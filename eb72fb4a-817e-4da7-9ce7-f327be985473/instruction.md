# October roster period: safe staffing audit for the Acute Medicine nurses at Marrowgate General

Mairead Colquhoun, head of nursing workforce at Marrowgate Health Trust, here. It is Thursday 29 October 2026.
The 28-day roster period that ran from 1 to 28 October has finished, and I need the safe staffing
audit for the Acute Medicine charge nurses and staff nurses at Marrowgate General done end to end: every
exceedance found and recorded, the period closed, each affected nurse told, and audit request SSA-1407
finished. The SS-4 limits changed part-way through the period, so mind which revision governs
which shift.

## What you have

You can reach these workforce systems as tools: the workforce workspace (the nurse register, the roster, the shift
actuals in two databases split at the revision date, the shift extension authorisations register, the register of SS-4 revisions, the findings
database for this period and the period-close records), the document library holding both revisions of
SS-4 and some staff side papers, the staffing compliance mailbox, the workforce tracker with the audit
request and the staffing incident reports, the nursing chat channels, the compliance calendar, and the site
operations incident log. Our shared staffing-compliance folder sits under data in your working directory: the field guide for
this audit, last week's roster export, printed copies of SS-4, photographs of ward timesheet pages,
scans of shift extension authorisations and the site manager's bleep-log voice notes. Other trust systems are
wired to you as well; they hold nothing for this audit.

## What I need

1. **Find every exceedance** for the in-scope nurses in the period: shift length, shift
   extensions, minimum rest and the 7, 14 and 28 day rolling-hours limits, each under the revision
   of SS-4 that applies to it.
2. **Record them.** One record per exceedance in this period's findings database, laid out precisely
   as the field guide sets out.
3. **Log the corrections.** Wherever a ward timesheet page, an extension authorisation or a bleep-log note
   overrode the shift actuals, file it in the actuals corrections register as the field guide sets out.
4. **Close the period** with the figures the field guide asks for.
5. **Tell the nurses.** One notice to each nurse with a finding, from the compliance mailbox, to the
   address the nurse register gives, as the field guide sets out.
6. **Follow up the incident reports.** Annotate the open staffing incident reports of the nurses you notified, as
   the field guide sets out.
7. **Finish the request.** Append the field guide's lines to SSA-1407 and move it to Done.

Finish by writing me a brief account covering: for each finding, which rule and which line of the table
set its limit; which revision you applied either side of the change and how you decided; every place
where you set aside what a record said (the roster, the shift actuals, the export, anything said in the
channels or the staff side papers) and which record you relied on instead; and how many findings, how many
minutes of excess in total and how many nurses you notified. Give every finding's limit, actual and excess in
minutes, say which other ways of settling the revision you ruled out and how you tested the rolling-hours limits,
and tell me what changed in the workforce systems while you worked and what you did about it, where a system refused,
limited or cut short what it returned and how you still got every record, and which corrections and incident report
annotations you filed. Write it as prose, without pasting tool output.

## Rules

- Write only to this period's findings database, the actuals corrections register, the October
  period-close record, the open incident reports of the nurses you notify, SSA-1407 and the compliance
  mailbox's outgoing notices. Everything else is read-only. Never delete or archive a record.
- SS-4 and the records it names decide every figure. A nurse's wish, a staff side view, a draft
  agreement or an export does not.
- Nothing leaves the office except the nurses' notices: do not reply in the channels, and do not mail
  anyone other than the nurses you are notifying.
- Work from the workforce systems as they stand while you work, not from the export.

## What the office relies on

The office works from what you leave in the systems: the findings, the corrections register, the period-close
record, the notices, the incident reports and the audit request. Your note explains how each figure was reached, so it
stands only when the records underneath it are right.
